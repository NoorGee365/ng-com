Our Identity

About NoorGee

Welcome to NoorGee, a central brand hub and digital ecosystem founded by Nooruddin. Our core identity stems from combining the dynamic reach of News Media with advanced IT Technologies, offering global consumers and professionals a unified doorway to tools, services, and online platforms.

Whether managing top-tier WooCommerce platforms in the United States and Pakistan, hosting free development tools for the web community, or organizing social development campaigns, NoorGee operates as a unified platform driven by digital excellence and social responsibility.

We strive to keep our services state-of-the-art and easy to navigate. Beyond commercial targets, our core vision highlights community empowerment, supporting local legal aids, small portfolios, and non-profit associations directly from our profits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our digital services, stores, and operations.

NoorGee is a centralized digital ecosystem and brand hub created by Nooruddin, combining e-commerce platforms, digital toolkits, informational blogs, professional services, and social welfare projects under one unified roof.
Currently, NoorGee operates active e-commerce stores in the United States (us.noorgee.com) and Pakistan (noorgee.pk), focusing on delivering curated consumer goods using WordPress and WooCommerce integrations.
Our IT Tool Site (it.noorgee.com) offers various digital utilities, including a native Urdu Keyboard Layout converter (UR), a professional Name Generator (NM), and a News Format Maker (NW) for media creators.
We run two dedicated blog portals to share smart shopping guides and consumer tips: blog.noorgee.com (English blog for US/Global consumers) and blog.noorgee.pk (Urdu blog for Pakistani consumers).
NoorGee provides legal consulting services through NG Law (noorgee.pk/law), portfolio website creation by Noor (web.noorgee.com), advanced custom web applications by Athar (noorgee.pk/dev), and event videography by Rizwan Elahi (nm.noorgee.pk).
KWA (kwa.com.pk) is NoorGee's voluntary partner dedicated to healthcare and education in Pakistan. You can read about our ongoing initiatives and contribute directly via the KWA portal.
